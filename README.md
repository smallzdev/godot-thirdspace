### What we learned

**1. NEVER organize files when you are working at the same time!**

We learned this the hard way... Before our scenes and GDScript files weren't organized into files, so they were all in the open. I (Victoria) decided to take the intiative to organize the files and we thought it wouldn't cause a merge conflict... well... unfortunately it did. 

I had to save a back up of my files and redownload the zip file :') 

**2. Pixel art!**

I believe this is also our first time doing pixel art! For me (Victoria), I learned more than I expected on pixel art! I thought that it would be the same as drawing on paper, but surprisingly it's not. If you drew like you did on normal paper on a pixel art canvas, it would look super messy. So, I learned that you have to be mindful of your brushstrokes and colour. 

**3. Time management**

I think Smallz had this under control. He commited to doing at least 1h25m everyday (very impressive!) and more. On the other hand... I underestimated how much 10 hours is... I got a huge majority of my work done over the weekend... this isn't very sustainable every week, so I really need to commit to third spaces everyday like Smallz. 

I look forward to the future weeks of third spaces and I'm excited to see what I learn about game development!

I also wonder how much I'll learn about software development. As seen with the merge conflict...

### Feature List
**Controls:**
- Jump (W, Space, or Up Arrow)
- Right (D, or Right Arrow)
- Left (A, or Left Arrow)
- Interact (E)

**Other Features**
- Checkpoints that change colour when activated
- Collectable coins
- 3 Life System
- Game Over Screen
- Going in the void that makes you respawn
- Harvest trees!
- More!

The rest of the art, was made by either one of us. Including the singular track as of now! I look forward to composing more tracks for our game!

Currently, the free assets pack is used as a place holder for now. We are looking to make our game entirely our own!

## What we learned

**1. Again, Time management**

Once again, even worse this time, I (Victoria) am submitting this super last minute. I thought I had learned my lesson from last week, but I clearly haven't. My time management was even worse this week! This week was a little bit more busier as school actually "started" for me. Last week, most of my week were just lessons to get us started with the school year, but this week I had 2 (technically 3) assessments. I got to skip out on one due to a field trip! 

Next week, I really need to space out my work more evenly. Although it would be great if to do ~one hour per week day, I think that's not for me. I enjoy focusing on one thing for a really long time, so I think I will try to do dedicate ~two days of the week day grinding third spaces and leaving the weekend for a lot of heavy work. If I could leave 5 hours for the weekend and split up 2.5/hrs a day on Sunday and Saturday that would be wonderful.

Unfortunately, I had to cancel a few things due to third spaces again... :')

Besides third spaces, I also really need to manage my time... university + post secondary stuff are all starting to start up soon and I need to make sure I get on top of things!

Smallz however was super disciplined as always! Congrats to him!!

**2. Communication**

Not sure if Smallz felt this way too (smallz here, i do), but I would have really liked to sit down and talk about the game. Most of the time we just send each other messages while were sleeping (14hr time difference) and read them during the morning. It feels a little awkward not knowing what I should focus on next and we're both guessing what we would like to implement.

**3. Subtle cues in game development**

After the release of week one, I showed my game to as many people as I could and waited to see their reaction. One of the people I showed it to has experience in developping games and said that I should add a particle effect after harvesting trees. This subtly tells the player that you earn money from harvesting trees. 

Showing this game to people who don't game much is also super interesting! Before week two, the game would say "It's Harvest Season!" and then teach you how to move. Some people who demo'd my game were not sure what to do when loaded in the game! I realized that it would be better to reverse the two texts instead. 

**4. GODOT!**

This section is a little different compared to the others (and somewhat obvious - but still think it's pretty cool and wanted to share), but what I noticed this week/end of last week was I started getting familliar with Godot. I heavily relied on guides during the beginning, but with a bit of knowledge I learned from the guides - I was able to apply that and a bit of logic to this week. It's pretty cool to see developpment. I hope to see how much I learn in the future...

**5. Composing**

Composing was completely new to me before third spaces. Before I only touched a bit of garage band when I was like 8, but now I was actually composing songs. The first track I added from last week was entirely improvised and came out much faster than I thought. 

The only music I touch is singing.. and like a bit of music theory I had to learn due to being in said music classes... and surprisingly it came in handy. Knowing a bit of key signatures helped more than I thought!

As for the second track I composed... that took soo much longer than the first one. This time, I was actually decided to compose for real. I was trying to find some game soundtracks I liked and take apart the sections and stitch them all together! One of my favourite soundtracks comes from Toby Fox (if that isn't painfully obvious), so I took a look on what songs I could sample from and came up with A Town Called Hometown. At first I was trying to find other game soundtracks (generally speaking) that had an autumn vibe and came up with hometown from Deltarune! 

At first, I tried sampling the main melody... but it came out super weird and eventually decided on sampling the bass instead. It came out super accurate and it also showed me... I really needed work on note reading... It took me much longer than I thought to read notes from sheet music. I then improvised the main melody, which turned out better than I thought... however I wanted to come up with a satisifying ending to the song... coming soon!

As for the next track I composed (level 2 soundtrack)... this one is probably my weakest one. I tried again to sample songs from Toby Fox, but this time was a lot more difficult than I thought. Probably due to the weird time signatures he uses.. and I'm not entirely sure how to make a whole note, quarter note etc. in the application I use. So the beginning took super long to compose... :') eventually I just decided to improvise the song. 

Another interesting fact: all my friends thought that the first track was better than my second one (the one I spent like 5x the amount of time on)... 

They told me that I should just go with what sounds nice instead of worrying about theory, so hopefully my second track is decent..
