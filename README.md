### What we learned

**1. NEVER organize files when you are working at the same time!**

We learned this the hard way... Before our scenes and GDScript files weren't organized into files, so they were all in the open. I (Victoria) decided to take the intiative to organize the files and we thought it wouldn't cause a merge conflict... well... unfortunately it did. 

I had to save a back up of my files and redownload the zip file :') 

**2. Pixel art!**

I believe this is also our first time doing pixel art! For me (Victoria), I learned more than I expected on pixel art! I thought that it would be the same as drawing on paper, but surprisingly it's not. If you drew like you did on normal paper on a pixel art canvas, it would look super messy. So, I learned that you have to be mindful of your brushstrokes and colour. 

**3. Time management**

I think Smallz had this under control. He commited to doing at least 1h25m everyday (very impressive!) and more. On the other hand... I underestimated how much 10 hours is... I got a huge majority of my work done over the weekend... this isn't very sustainable every week, so I really need to commit to third spaces everyday like Smallz. 

I look forward to the future weeks of third spaces and I'm excited to see what I learn about game development!

I also wonder how much I'll learn about software development. As seen with the merge conflict...

### Feature List
**Controls:**
- Jump (W, Space, or Up Arrow)
- Right (D, or Right Arrow)
- Left (A, or Left Arrow)
- Interact (E)

**Other Features**
- Checkpoints that change colour when activated
- Collectable coins
- 3 Life System
- Game Over Screen
- Going in the void that makes you respawn
- Harvest trees!
- More!

### Who made the art?
The frog (player character) & terrain in the game is from a free assets pack, which is under public domain (https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/pixel-adventure-1).

The rest of the art, was made by either one of us. Including the singular track as of now! I look forward to composing more tracks for our game!

Currently, the free assets pack is used as a place holder for now. We are looking to make our game entirely our own!
